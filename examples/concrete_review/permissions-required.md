# Source authorization required

No normative ACI or ASCE rule is implemented or ingested. The catalog contains
public bibliographic facts, not permission, adoption evidence, or a standards
corpus. No restricted PDF, publisher worked example, mirror, or errata content
was used to create the mechanics calculation or its original fixtures.

Before implementing any named standards rule, obtain written authorization for:

1. The exact standard, edition, unit edition, amendments, errata snapshot and
   supported provision locators, distinguishing normative text and commentary.
2. Reading and automated processing of those materials.
3. AI use and creation of derived executable implementations and test fixtures.
4. Redistribution of the intended code, excerpts (if any), fixtures and reports.
5. Transmission to each named model provider, including retention/access terms,
   if any transmission is intended. No such transmission is currently enabled.
6. The specific public repository, license, developer-preview distribution and
   end-user population. An external checker's terms must be reviewed separately.

For ACI, the [published Board policy](https://www.concrete.org/newsandevents/news/newsdetail.aspx?f=51751802)
requires a written exception from its Executive Vice President for the relevant
AI uses. ASCE's [terms](https://www.asce.org/about-asce/terms-conditions/) separately
restrict AI and other reuse of licensed materials. Purchase, membership,
subscription, or a login does not establish these rights.

Suggested owner request (not sent): “Please confirm in writing whether the
specific provisions and uses listed above are authorized, identifying every
condition, permitted distribution, attribution, revocation rule and expiry.
We will keep the associated checks disabled until rights and independent
verification are established.”

There is no switch accepting a user-supplied `authorized: true` flag, no automatic
email, purchase, account creation, or external checker call. Source authorization
does not by itself establish implementation correctness or code adoption.
